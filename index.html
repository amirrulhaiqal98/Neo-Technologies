Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is a laptop trade-in?

    Laptop trade-in is a process of exchanging your used laptop for credit, cash value, or with another laptop.

  • How do I know the trade-in value of my laptop?

    Our trade-in experts assess the value based on factors such as the laptop's brand, model, age, condition, and current market demand. Visit our website or contact us for a personalized trade-in quote.

  • What should I include when trading in my laptop?

    Please bring your laptop along with its charger and any relevant accessories. It's also recommended to back up and remove any personal data before trading it in.

  • What happens to my personal data on the traded-in laptop?

    Back up your files, sign out of personal accounts and remove your personal data before handing over your laptop. Remove account or activation locks that would prevent inspection. If you need help, ask our team about the available options before you sell.

  • How do I receive the trade-in credit or payment?

    After inspection, our team confirms the final offer for your agreement. Ask us to confirm the payment method and expected timing for your device before proceeding; these are not a fixed turnaround guarantee.

  • Is Tech Recycle part of Mechanize?

    Yes. Tech Recycle is operated by Mechanize. Our shop is in Sunway Mentari, Petaling Jaya. Find our used laptop selling guide on Mechanize's website, or read panduan jual laptop terpakai dalam Bahasa Melayu.

  • Can I arrange pickup or send my laptop by courier?

    Yes, pickup and courier delivery are available by arrangement. Contact our team first to confirm your location, availability, any charges and packing instructions. An initial quote is subject to inspection and your agreement to the final offer.
